history: >-
  Lost to history remnants suddenly appeared one day among the ruins, a weak race that was looked down towards,
  with many to this day still considering either disgusting or worthless.

  In earlier eras their numbers were low, easy to kill in a dangerous world were their casualties from monsters were unnaturally high,
  and their presence seemed to bring danger to them. Yet for how easy it is to kill them they kept appearing again, and again.
  Which won them the nickname of cockroaches, never consider a remain dead until you watch their cold dead corpse.

  The dangers they seem to bring made them historicaly an outcast race no many welcomed, with many remaining in the ruins away from unwelcoming eyes

  And through evolution, throuth blessing of gods recognizing families, other races began to form and branch out, elves, dwarves, and other remnant races bearing their blood while others formed from adaption to other environments and cross breeding with other races. 

  In the current era it's nearly impossible to find a true pure blooded remnant, causing the race to have traits of other races. With 2 other "pure" remnant races appearing to share the struggle.

  Though weak they are incredibly difficult to take out, vast in numbers and varied in powers that develop incredible fast.
