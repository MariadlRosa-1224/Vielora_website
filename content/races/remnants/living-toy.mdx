summary: >-
  Living Toys are animated dolls, plushies, toys, or crafted companions brought
  to life through mana. As long as their core remains intact, their bodies can be
  repaired, modified, and even rebuilt.

description: >-
  Living Toys take the appearance of toys, dolls, plushies, and other crafted
  objects. Generally they are small, but their size is based on their creator and
  the type of toy they were made from. Aside from the usual appearance of the toy
  they are based on, they have a small core incrusted somewhere in their body or
  worn as an accessory.

weaknesses:

  - id: soulless
    title: "Soulless"
    description: >-
      For good or bad, most Living Toys are unaffected by soul effects. This can
      protect them from certain abilities, but it also prevents them from
      benefiting from effects that require a soul.

history: >-
  Living Toys are a very old race, said to have first been created in the times
  of humans, when powerful mages started experimenting with mana and giving life
  to inanimate objects, such as toys.

  This practice, although very rare and only done by experienced mages, is still
  done nowadays. Toys are usually made as companions for children of rich
  families or as loyal servants of a fun size. This gives them a kind and
  playful nature that, added to their cuteness, makes them a well-liked race.
  Despite their origin, they are treated normally.

  Some Living Toys, however, are made with an actual soul. These ones do not
  remember their previous lives or only have very vague memories. This practice
  is heavily prohibited and taboo, making it very unlikely to find a toy made in
  this way.
