description: >-
  Nyphen were made to look mostly human. Their great difference are their wings, colors, halos and how beautiful they always look. Their wings can vary a lot in shape, size and color, except black. Many nyphen have small wings and feathers in different parts of their body. Still a single pair of full wings is what is most commonly seen. Having 2 or 3 pairs is incredibly rare, a sight only reserved to the most powerful of nyphen. But anything beyond 3 is unheard, only a thing of legends, of divine beings and of monsters.
