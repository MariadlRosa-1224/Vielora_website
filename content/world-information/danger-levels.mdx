# Danger Levels


This is a list of the different levels of dangers tied to the creatures of the world, the danger level is an explicit indication of the rank of a creatures and is a way to differentiate the danger of a individual creature within its rank

## N/A
There isn’t enough information to categorize them.



## E

> There's no danger in being around them no matter the age or condiction of the person.

#### 0

They don’t suppose any danger at all, no matter what they do, they won’t harm a person nor have the capabilities to do it.

## D

>They are considered mostly inoffensive, a single person with average physical or magical capabilities can deal with them, but precausion is still reccomended.

#### *1*

small and noticeably easy to kill animals without traits that make them more dangerous than that.
#### *2*
Equivalent or similar to the average human without training.

#### *3*

Equivalent to a human with training, such as athletes or animals that are dangerous in most circumstances, such as bears, lions, wolves, etc. Without major magical traits that make them more dangerous than that.

## C

> Is not advised for the average civilian to deal with them alone. But encountering one in the wild isn't considered dangerous if they are left unbothered.

#### *4*

They can destroy tough materials such as stone, steel, etc with relative ease and could destroy a wall made of these materials if it is not too thick. and their capabilities surpass even the strongest humans, but not necessarily by a lot.
#### *5*

They can cause a lot of property damage if they are let to, but aren’t considered dangerous to civilization if dealt with in time.

#### *6*

Can destroy small buildings such as houses with relative ease with either their physical or magical capabilities. Their capabilities greatly surpass those of humans.

## B
> Trained individuals are to even try to deal with one of the creatures of this category. Avoiding them is greatly suggested and there is no guarantee to survive an encounter with them.

#### *7*

They could destroy medium-sized buildings such as complexes or factories without much difficulty.

#### *8*

Could destroy large buildings such as skyscrapers.
#### *9*

Could destroy a city block or an kill an equivalent amount of people without major difficulties.

## A
> It requires a whole army just to deal with a single creature of this rank. Only individuals of particularly great power can survive hostile encounters with them alone, you must no in any circumstance try to fight them if you appreciate your life
#### *10*

Can destroy a town.
#### *11*

Can destroy a small city.
#### *12*

Can destroy a large city with great defenses.

## S-

> Only the sight of a creature of this rank or higher mandates the complete evacuation of all civilizations in the vinsinity, even the biggest of armies won't be likely to defeat them.

#### *13*

They have the capabilities to reform the whole landscape of an area multiple cities away from them or have capabilities that make them be considered equally as dangerous.
## S+

#### *14*

If they want or have the opportunity, they can destroy whole landscapes and countries
